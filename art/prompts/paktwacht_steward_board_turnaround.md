## Prompt

> Create an image. The first attached image is the APPROVED PORTRAIT of THE STEWARD of the Paktwacht castle ruin, an old key keeper in my original fantasy tactics RPG 'Hollow Crown'. The second attached image is an approved BOARD-FIGURE TURNAROUND of another character: copy its FORMAT and STYLE exactly (landscape 3:2, three views side by side: FRONT, SIDE looking to the RIGHT, BACK; wide spacing between the views, same scale, feet on one baseline, neutral A-pose with arms angled down and away from the body and hands open, flat light-grey background #E6E6E6, no shadows, no text, no labels). Do NOT copy that character's clothes, colours or the magenta robe - only format and drawing style. Draw THE STEWARD as a STRONGLY SIMPLIFIED round chibi board figure for a small 3D mobile game piece about 120 pixels tall: about 2.5 heads tall, big round head, large clean shapes, big flat colour areas, one shadow tone, slightly thicker dark-brown outlines, no fabric texture, no small accessories. His BUILD must read at a glance: SLIM and THIN, narrow body, thin arms and legs, a gentle stoop with the head pushed slightly forward (still a symmetric A-pose). Keep him instantly recognisable from the portrait, about 68 years old: thin straight GREY hair combed back flat with a few wisps standing up, big ears, small droopy kind brown eyes with bags under them, long slightly hooked nose, short grey stubble as one flat shape, tired gentle smile, pale weathered skin. Clothes: a long faded DARK-GREEN (#4F5E48) wool vest reaching to mid-thigh with simple toggles, worn over a CREAM linen shirt with rolled sleeves and brown patches on the elbows, dark brown trousers, simple brown shoes; a big iron RING OF OLD KEYS (three or four big simple keys) hanging from his belt on his left hip. TEAM CLOTH: a NECKERCHIEF tied at his throat AND a wide cloth BELT SASH around his waist (the key ring hangs from it), both in ONE flat pure MAGENTA (#FF00FF), no shading, no gradient, no highlights, like a chroma-key mask. Nothing else is magenta; vest and shirt keep their normal colours. NO WEAPON and nothing in his hands: no key in the hand, no staff, hands open and empty.
