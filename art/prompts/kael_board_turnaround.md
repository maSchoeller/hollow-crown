## Stufe 1 – vereinfachen (Eingabe: Porträt-Turnaround)

> Create an image. The attached turnaround sheet shows KAEL from my fantasy tactics RPG 'Hollow Crown'. Redraw the SAME turnaround sheet (FRONT view, SIDE view, BACK view, side by side, same scale, feet on one baseline, neutral A-pose, flat light-grey background #E6E6E6, no shadows, no text) but as a STRONGLY SIMPLIFIED LOW-DETAIL version for a small in-game 3D mobile figure that is only about 120 pixels tall on a phone screen. Keep the character instantly recognisable (dark brown hair with short low ponytail, amber eyes, light stubble, steel breastplate, ONE big rounded pauldron on his LEFT shoulder, ember-orange (#E8743B) sash with hanging end, grey cloak on his back, brown gloves and boots, sword sheathed at the left hip) and keep the round chibi proportions (about 2.5 heads tall, big round head). SIMPLIFY: large clean shapes and big flat colour areas, smooth simple cloak with only a few large zig-zag tears instead of many frayed holes, plain smooth armour without dents, scratches or engravings, hair as a few big chunky locks, at most two belts and two buckles, no stitching, no fabric texture, no small accessories, no dirt or weathering. Clean soft cel shading with only one shadow tone, slightly thicker dark-brown outlines. Style like a polished low-poly hand-painted mobile game figure (Clash Royale, Kingdom Rush 3D). The side view must show the pauldron and the sheathed sword on his left side.

## Stufe 2 – Waffe entfernen, Teamtuch als Maske (Eingabe: Ergebnis von Stufe 1)

> Create an image. Redraw this exact turnaround sheet (same character KAEL, same simplified low-detail style, same three views FRONT / SIDE / BACK, same A-pose, same scale and baseline, same flat light-grey background, no text) with exactly these two changes: 1) REMOVE the sword and its scabbard completely - no weapon anywhere on the body, the left hip just shows the belt and sash. 2) Paint his CLOAK and the HOOD/COLLAR SCARF around his neck in ONE flat pure MAGENTA colour (#FF00FF) with NO shading, NO gradient and NO highlights, like a chroma-key mask, keeping the cloak's shape and its few big zig-zag tears. Everything else (hair, face, steel breastplate and left pauldron, ember-orange sash, brown gloves and boots, dark trousers) stays exactly as it is, with its normal colours and shading.

Für neue Figuren beide Stufen in einem Prompt zusammenfassen (vereinfacht, ohne Waffe, Teamtuch in Magenta).
