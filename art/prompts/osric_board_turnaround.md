## Prompt

> Create an image. The first attached image is the APPROVED PORTRAIT of OSRIC DUNN, a slippery job broker in my original fantasy tactics RPG 'Hollow Crown'. The second attached image is an approved BOARD-FIGURE TURNAROUND of another character: copy its FORMAT and STYLE exactly (landscape 3:2, three views side by side: FRONT, SIDE looking to the RIGHT, BACK; wide spacing between the views, same scale, feet on one baseline, neutral A-pose with arms angled down and away from the body and hands open, flat light-grey background #E6E6E6, no shadows, no text, no labels). Do NOT copy that character's clothes, colours or the magenta robe - only format and drawing style. Draw OSRIC as a STRONGLY SIMPLIFIED round chibi board figure for a small 3D mobile game piece about 120 pixels tall: about 2.5 heads tall, big round head, large clean shapes, big flat colour areas, one shadow tone, slightly thicker dark-brown outlines, no fabric texture, no small accessories. His BUILD must read at a glance: HEAVY and ROUND, a big round belly, narrow sloping shoulders, short stubby legs - clearly wider and rounder than a soldier. Keep him instantly recognisable from the portrait: balding head with a shiny bald top and a few dark strands combed over it, short dark hair at the sides, soft double chin, small half-lidded dark eyes, thin pencil moustache, oily closed smile with one gold tooth, ruddy button nose, pale skin. Clothes: a long MUSTARD-YELLOW (#C9A23F) merchant coat reaching below the knees, open at the front, with a big chunky BROWN FUR COLLAR over the shoulders and slightly frayed cream cuffs; a cream waistcoat over the belly; dark trousers, brown boots; gold rings drawn as simple gold dots on his fingers; a small brown coin purse hanging from his belt on his left hip. TEAM CLOTH: a neck CRAVAT with a short hanging end AND a wide waist SASH wrapped around his belly instead of a belt, both in ONE flat pure MAGENTA (#FF00FF), no shading, no gradient, no highlights, like a chroma-key mask. Nothing else is magenta; the coat and fur keep their normal colours. NO WEAPON and nothing in his hands: no ledger, no book, no purse in the hand, hands open and empty.

## Abnahme

- Schwer und rund mit großem Bauch, schmalen Schultern, kurzen Beinen – deutlich anders als Söldner und Soldat ✓; Halbglatze mit Strähnen, Doppelkinn, Bleistiftbart, Goldzahn, Leberfleck, rote Knollennase ✓; senfgelber Mantel mit braunem Pelzkragen, Ringe ✓; Geldbeutel am Gürtel links ✓; Halstuch und Bauchschärpe flach Magenta ✓; keine Waffe, Hauptbuch nicht in der Hand, Hände leer ✓; kein Text ✓.
- Kleinigkeit: Die Schärpe liegt hinten über dem Mantel (vorn darunter); für die Textur unerheblich, das Teamtuch wird dadurch hinten etwas größer.
