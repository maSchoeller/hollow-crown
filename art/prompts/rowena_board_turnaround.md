## Prompt 1

> Create an image. The first attached image is the APPROVED PORTRAIT of DAME ROWENA, a knight of the Silver Lance in my original fantasy tactics RPG 'Hollow Crown'. The second attached image is an approved BOARD-FIGURE TURNAROUND of another character: copy its FORMAT and STYLE exactly (landscape 3:2, three views side by side: FRONT, SIDE looking to the RIGHT, BACK; wide spacing between the views, same scale, feet on one baseline, neutral A-pose with arms angled down and away from the body, flat light-grey background #E6E6E6, no shadows, no text, no labels). Do NOT copy that character's body, clothes, colours or the magenta sash - only format and drawing style. Draw ROWENA as a STRONGLY SIMPLIFIED round chibi board figure for a small 3D mobile game piece about 120 pixels tall: about 2.5 heads tall, big round head, large clean shapes, big flat colour areas, one shadow tone, slightly thicker dark-brown outlines, smooth armour without dents or engravings, at most two belts, no small accessories. BUILD: tall and slim for a chibi, straight noble posture. Keep her instantly recognisable from the portrait: BARE HEAD, NO helmet on the head; long platinum-blonde hair (#E8DDB5) pulled into ONE thick HIGH BRAID that starts at the crown and hangs down her back to the hips, drawn as a few big chunky segments (clearly visible in side and back view), a dark-blue hair band at the top, a few big soft strands framing the face; large blue eyes, determined small mouth, fair skin. Smooth rounded silver plate armour (#C9D1DC) with gold trim (#D9A931): round pauldrons, breastplate, armoured arms and greaves, dark-brown gloves and one brown belt. SIGNATURE: her silver knight's HELMET with a dark visor slit and two small white FEATHERED WINGS on its sides, held in her LEFT hand at hip height like in the portrait, the left arm still in the A-pose angle, so that there is a CLEAR GAP of background between the helmet and her hip/leg in front and back view (the helmet must not touch the body). TEAM CLOTH: a cape on her back from the shoulders to the knees AND the long tabard panel in front and back hanging from the belt, both in ONE flat pure MAGENTA (#FF00FF), no shading, no gradient, no emblem, like a chroma-key mask. Nothing else is magenta; the braid lies over the cape in the back view. NO lance, NO weapon, NO sword, NO shield: her right hand is open and empty.

## Prompt 2 (Korrektur)

> Very good, keep everything (style, colours, armour, magenta cape and tabard, face, helmet design, size and format) and fix only these points so the three views match for a 3D reconstruction: 1) The BRAID must be in the SAME place in all three views: it starts at the crown and hangs straight down the CENTRE OF HER BACK over the cape, like in the back view. In the FRONT view the braid is therefore hidden behind her head and body - do NOT show it hanging over or beside her shoulder; in the side view it hangs down behind her back. 2) Make the hair volume a bit smaller and simpler: fewer, bigger strands, the face framed by two short soft strands. 3) The HELMET is held in her LEFT hand, and her left arm is the arm on the FAR side in the SIDE view (she looks to the right, so we see her right side): in the side view only a small part of the helmet and one wing may peek out from behind her hip, it must NOT be drawn in front of her belly or chest. In the front and back views keep the helmet where it is, but with a small visible gap of background between the helmet and the cape/hip. Same image format, no text.
