## Prompt

> Create an image. TITLE SCREEN key art for my original fantasy tactics RPG 'Hollow Crown' (mobile, PORTRAIT format, tall 2:3). The attached images are APPROVED references: image 1 = KAEL (character), image 2 = RHEN (character, use only the outfit), image 3 = the PAKTWACHT castle ruin (location), image 4 = the company's campfire camp (mood and colours). STYLE: warm, friendly, rounded cartoon painting with soft outlines and simple cel-like shading; the two characters in exactly the round chibi style of images 1 and 2 (big round heads, short rounded bodies); bright but warm night colours; NOT photorealistic, NOT gritty. SCENE: night in late summer on a grassy hill. In the LOWER HALF a crackling campfire; KAEL sits on a log on the far side of the fire, facing the viewer, lit warmly by the fire, calm and protective, his longsword leaning beside him, ash-grey cloak and ember-orange sash. RHEN sits on a log in the foreground with the BACK to the viewer, seen from behind and slightly from the side, the night-blue tactician coat with silver trim, HOOD UP so no hair and no face are visible, a leather map satchel at the side, an ember-orange scarf end hanging out. In the MIDDLE behind them on a far hill the silhouette of the PAKTWACHT castle ruin with its broken round tower. In the starry night sky above, far away, the dark silhouette of a large DRAGON gliding across the sky, a faint ember glow on its wings. TOP THIRD: calm, empty starry sky with no important details, kept free for the game logo. A few sparks rise from the fire. No text, no letters, no logo, no frame, no border.

**Korrektur:**

> Very good composition - keep everything exactly as it is (layout, sky, dragon, ruin, fire, Rhen from behind, empty top third, colours).
