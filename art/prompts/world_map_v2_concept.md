## Prompt 1

> Create an image (portrait 2:3, 1024 x 1536): a hand-painted illustrated fantasy world map of the kingdom of Valmora, seen from a high bird's-eye view (almost top-down, north up), for a cozy but serious tactics RPG. Use the FIRST attached image as the exact layout: light blue = sea and lakes, beige = land, grey triangles = mountains (snowy tips = snow peaks), green dots = forests, blue lines = rivers, the striped green ellipse in the south = marshland, the brown block by the lake = the capital city, the brown block under the middle ridge = a castle, the red dot at the top = a glowing volcanic crater temple. Keep the coastline exactly as in the guide: a deep narrow fjord in the north-west, a wide river estuary in the middle with a small river island, a long peninsula pointing west below it, a separate island off the coast, a bay with a river delta and marsh in the south, small rocky islets. Seasons run from south to north: the southern borderland in late summer (green meadows, golden fields, a big river valley, a dense forest), the middle highland in autumn (rust and ochre plateaus, a canyon with waterfalls, copper-coloured hills, cliffs and beaches on the coast), the north in winter (a frozen lake, a snowy capital with golden roofs, snowy forests, glaciers and a huge dragon mountain range with one dark crater glowing orange). Small painted landmarks are welcome: the castle built into the southern flank of the middle mountain ridge with terraces and fields in front of it, the capital on the frozen lake, a harbour town at the estuary, small villages, a ship near the island. Use the SECOND attached image only for painting style, palette and mood. No text, no labels, no borders, no compass rose, no UI.
