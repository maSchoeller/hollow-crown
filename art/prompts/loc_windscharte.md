I will ask for several locations in this chat; all are in act 2, which plays in AUTUMN in the Ironmark highlands (heather, orange and rust-red leaves, brown grass, single bare trees, cool clear highland blue). Location 1: the WINDSCHARTE (Wind Notch), the crest of an old mountain pass road in a grey stone ridge, EARLY MORNING, clear sky: a dry old river valley about as wide as a small road runs from the foreground into the distance between steep grey cliffs, with pale smooth river stones and brown autumn grass on its floor. Beyond the notch the land opens into a wide autumn plain with heather, a few orange trees and a winding road. Strong wind: grass bent, a few leaves and a torn ember-orange pennant flying from one ballista. Cool soft morning light from the left, long shadows. Wide, windy and tense, but warm and beautiful.
