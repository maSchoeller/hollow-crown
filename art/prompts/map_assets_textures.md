## sheet4

> Create a square image 1024x1024: a texture sheet of four seamless tileable game textures in a 2x2 grid, separated by a thin pure white gap in the exact middle, no labels, no text, no frame. Each quadrant is a flat, straight-on, evenly lit texture with no perspective and no cast shadow, hand-painted, chunky rounded forms, matching the warm stone in the attached reference pictures. Top left: wall-top flagstones, large irregular worn pinkish-beige sandstone slabs, thin darker gaps. Top right: castle wall face, coursed ashlar of chunky rectangular blocks in 4 rows, warm mid-brown stone with darker mortar and slight colour variation between blocks. Bottom left: top of a rock massif seen from above, large angular facets of grey-brown stone with crisp dark cracks, faceted planes clearly lighter and darker, a hint of moss in a few cracks. Bottom right: rock cliff face, stacked angular ledges of darker grey-brown stone with crisp shadows between them. Every quadrant tiles seamlessly at its edges.

## sheet5

> Create a square image 1024x1024: a texture sheet of four seamless tileable game textures in a 2x2 grid, separated by a thin pure white gap in the exact middle, no labels, no text, no frame. Each quadrant is a flat, straight-on, evenly lit texture with no perspective and no cast shadow, hand-painted, chunky rounded forms, matching the warm stone in the attached reference pictures. Top left: light honey-golden sandstone ashlar, chunky rounded blocks in about 5 courses, bright warm highlights on each block, darker mortar lines, for towers and battlements. Top right: heavy oak gate planks, vertical warm brown boards with dark seams and wood grain. Bottom left: castle courtyard ground, calm beige packed earth with a few pebbles and worn flat stones, low contrast. Bottom right: dark cool grey worn flagstone floor of a vault, large cracked slabs with thin dark gaps. Every quadrant tiles seamlessly at its edges.
