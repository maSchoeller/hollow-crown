Nachricht 1 (mit Anhang):

```text
Create an image. The attached image is the APPROVED dialogue portrait of PRINCESS SERAPHINE for my original fantasy tactics RPG 'Hollow Crown' (round-chibi cartoon portrait style). Same character, same pose, crop, outfit, hands, locket and colours, same line and cel shading, same size and position in the square. Change only brows, eyes, mouth and shoulders to ANGRY: brows pulled down hard toward the nose, eyes narrowed, mouth open with clenched teeth showing, shoulders pulled up; she is furious and hurt at the same time. Square 1:1, FULLY TRANSPARENT background (PNG with real alpha channel), no floor, no shadow, no text, no frame.
```

Nachricht 2:

```text
Very good. Now the SAD version, again based on the FIRST attached approved portrait (not on the angry one): same character, same pose, crop, outfit, hands, locket and colours, same line and cel shading, same size and position in the square. Change only brows, eyes, mouth and shoulders to SAD: brows up in the middle, eyes looking down with one small tear on the lower lid, mouth turned down and slightly trembling, shoulders dropped. Square 1:1, FULLY TRANSPARENT background (PNG with real alpha channel), no floor, no shadow, no text, no frame.
```
