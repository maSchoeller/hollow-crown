## Prompt

> Create an image. The first four attached character portraits (KAEL, BRENNA, MIRA, TOBIN) are the APPROVED STYLE REFERENCES for my fantasy tactics RPG 'Hollow Crown' ('Round Chibi Cartoon'). Match their style exactly, especially how their weapons and gear are drawn. The fifth attached image is an already approved signature-weapon icon from the same series; match its icon style, outline, shading and detail level exactly. Draw one game inventory icon in the SAME style and format: chunky, slightly oversized, toy-like proportions with rounded tips and thick simple shapes, soft dark-brown outline (a bit bolder than in the portraits), simple glossy cel shading, warm colors, light from the top-left; square 1:1, the single item centered and filling about 80% of the canvas; no hands, no character, no frame, no border, no text or letters, no ground shadow, no background scenery; FULLY TRANSPARENT background (PNG with real alpha channel); readable at 48x48 pixels (bold silhouette, no thin details). DETAIL: rich (legendary or signature item): detailed engravings, ornaments, wrapped cloth, small gems, worn texture; a unique heroic design clearly more special than a normal item. ORIENTATION: long axis diagonal from bottom-left to top-right, tip at the top-right; make the shaft thick enough to read at small size. ITEM: 'Heirloom', the signature lance of TOBIN (the young farmer's son in the references): an old family spear made from a farm heritage - a sturdy, slightly weathered ash-wood shaft with a worn leather grip wrap, an iron leaf-shaped spearhead forged from an old plough blade with a faint wheat-ear engraving, a small brass collar with a simple family mark, and a short faded green-and-straw-colored cloth tied below the head.
