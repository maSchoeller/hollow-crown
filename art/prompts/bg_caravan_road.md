## Prompt

> Create an image. The attached image is an approved visual-novel background from my original fantasy tactics RPG "Hollow Crown" - match its painted anime film background style exactly (same brushwork, same level of detail, same lighting quality), but paint a NEW location. Visual-novel dialogue background, widescreen landscape 16:9, no characters, no people, no animals in the foreground, no text. Scene: a merchant caravan on a dirt road in late summer, late afternoon, warm golden light from the low sun on the left (south-west), long soft shadows. Three or four covered merchant wagons with canvas tops (cream and faded red canvas), wooden wheels, crates and barrels, standing on a rutted dirt road that leads from the foreground into the distance in the centre of the image. On both sides ripe golden wheat fields, on the right a dark green forest edge, gentle hills and a blue sky with a few warm clouds in the background, pollen and floating dust sparkling in the light. Composition: the road and the main wagon are in the CENTRAL THIRD of the image (portrait-safe centre crop), the sides can be simpler; keep the lower third calm and uncluttered because character portraits and a text box will stand in front. Painted, slightly soft and slightly desaturated so characters stand out. Palette: wheat gold, ochre, lush green, warm blue sky.
