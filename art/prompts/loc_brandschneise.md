> Very good. Next location - same style and format (16:9, no people, no animals, no text, no logos; important content in the central third; lower third calm). Location 2: the FIREBREAK at BRANDHANG, the SAME highland village as in the third attached image, but now on the EVENING of a wildfire: the village sits on a middle terrace of a hillside built in three levels, with stone-and-timber cottages, haystacks and fences; above it on the upper terrace a dark autumn forest and grain fields, below it fields and a pasture. From the forest at the top (the far back) an orange wildfire is coming down, with glowing smoke and drifting ash flakes in a grey-violet sky. In the centre on the edge of the village terrace stands a small old stone shrine, and in front of the shrine a fresh wide strip of torn-up dark earth and grey ash cuts across the hillside like a scar: the firebreak that will stop the fire. Earthen ramps and stone stairs link the terraces. Warm orange firelight against cool evening blue. Dangerous and dramatic, but still warm, soft and readable, not grim.
