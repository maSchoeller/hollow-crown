- **Dateien:** `art/turnarounds/kael/sheet.png` (1536 × 1024, RGB, Original); daraus freigestellt `front.png`, `side.png`, `back.png` (je 1024 × 1024, Hintergrund `#E2E1E1`)
- **Datum:** 2026-10-23
- **Werkzeug:** ChatGPT (Bildgenerierung), gesteuert über Claude in Chrome; Chat „Create Character Turnaround Sheet“
- **Referenzen:** `art/mockups/characters-s1/kael.png` (Porträt), `art/mockups/style-2026-10/round_chibi_kael.png` (Stil)
- **Zweck:** Eingabe für den Bild-zu-3D-Vergleich Tripo gegen Meshy (`concept/23-art-direction/decision-log.md`)
- **Iterationen:** 1

## Prompt

> Create an image. The attached images are the APPROVED STYLE REFERENCES for KAEL from my original fantasy tactics RPG 'Hollow Crown' (first image: his approved portrait; second image: style sheet). I need a CHARACTER TURNAROUND SHEET that will be used as input for image-to-3D model generation of the in-game 3D figure. FORMAT: landscape 3:2, three full-body views of the SAME character side by side at exactly the same scale and height, feet on the same baseline: FRONT view, SIDE view (his left side, facing right), BACK view. Neutral relaxed A-pose (arms slightly away from the body, hands open, legs slightly apart), orthographic-like flat view, no perspective, no foreshortening. Plain flat light-grey background (#E6E6E6), even soft studio light, no cast shadows, no floor, no text, no labels, no logo. STYLE: exactly the 'Round Chibi Cartoon' of the references - about 2.5 heads tall, big ROUND head, large expressive amber eyes, soft dark-brown outlines, simple glossy cel shading, rounded toy-like forms, but with rich professional detail in the materials (stitching, buckles, metal dents, cloth folds) like a high-quality mobile game hero figure. CHARACTER: KAEL, 38, mercenary captain: dark brown hair in a short low ponytail, light stubble, tired but kind amber eyes, small scar through his left eyebrow, dented plain steel breastplate with a scratched-off emblem, ONE big rounded pauldron on his LEFT shoulder only, dark grey sleeves, brown leather gloves and belts, dark trousers, brown boots, an ash-grey frayed cloak hanging behind his back (cloak must be clearly visible in side and back view), an ember-orange (#E8743B) sash around the waist with a hanging end. NO weapon in his hands (the longsword is sheathed at his left hip in a simple brown scabbard). Calm, friendly expression.

## Nachbearbeitung

Die drei Ansichten an den Hintergrundlücken zwischen den Figuren getrennt (Spalten 28–562, 568–964, 992–1521) und mittig auf quadratische Flächen in Hintergrundfarbe gesetzt.

## Abnahme (Entwurf)

- Stil trifft Porträt und Stil-Mockup ✓, Detailgrad hoch (Nähte, Schnallen, Dellen, ausgefranster Umhang) ✓, gleiche Größe und Grundlinie ✓, A-Pose ✓, kein Text ✓.
- **Abweichung:** Die Seitenansicht zeigt Schwert und Schärpe der linken Seite, aber kein Schulterstück. Fällt das Modell an der Schulter unsauber aus, Seitenansicht nachbessern.
- Finale Abnahme durch den Produktinhaber steht aus.
