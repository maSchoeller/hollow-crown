> Create an image, 1536x1024 (landscape): an asset sheet of N separate game props on a plain light neutral background, seen from a high 3/4 camera about 35 degrees from straight down, south side towards the viewer, rounded chunky low-poly toy diorama, hand-painted textures, warm light, professional studio quality, no text, no people, evenly spaced. Same style as the attached picture. Do not use strong blue, strong red or strong green on the props.
