## Prompt

### Nachricht 1

> Create an image. The first attached image is the APPROVED PORTRAIT of LUCAN, captain of a mercenary company in my original fantasy tactics RPG 'Hollow Crown'. The second attached image is an approved BOARD-FIGURE TURNAROUND of another character: copy its FORMAT and STYLE exactly (landscape 3:2, three views side by side: FRONT, SIDE looking to the RIGHT, BACK; wide spacing between the views, same scale, feet on one baseline, neutral A-pose with arms angled down and away from the body and hands open, flat light-grey background #E6E6E6, no shadows, no text, no labels). Do NOT copy that character's clothes, colours or the magenta robe - only format and drawing style. Draw LUCAN as a STRONGLY SIMPLIFIED round chibi board figure for a small 3D mobile game piece about 120 pixels tall: about 2.5 heads tall, big round head, short rounded body, large clean shapes, big flat colour areas, one shadow tone, slightly thicker dark-brown outlines, no fabric texture, no small accessories. Keep him instantly recognisable from the portrait: well-kept dark brown hair a bit too long, tied into a short low ponytail with a few strands at the temples; calm amber eyes, refined face, small scar through the right eyebrow, faint dry smile; LEAN, upright noble bearing (not bulky). Clothes: a royal-green knight's surcoat over a plain smooth steel breastplate, dark charcoal under-collar, ONE big rounded steel pauldron with a thin gold rim on his LEFT shoulder only, a thick cream-white FUR COLLAR around his shoulders, a dark-green cloak hanging down his back to the calves with only a few big simple folds, brown leather gloves, dark trousers, brown boots, one brown belt. TEAM CLOTH: a wide waist SASH tied at his right hip with two long hanging ends to the knees, in ONE flat pure MAGENTA (#FF00FF), no shading, no gradient, no highlights, like a chroma-key mask. Nothing else is magenta; the surcoat, cloak and fur keep their normal colours. NO WEAPON anywhere: no sword, no scabbard, nothing in his hands. The side view must show the cloak and the sash end; the left pauldron is hidden in the right-side view.

### Nachricht 2

> Very good. Create an image: redraw this exact turnaround sheet (same character, same style, same three views, same A-pose, scale, baseline and background, no text) with ONE change: his signature piece, the ONE rounded steel PAULDRON on his LEFT shoulder, must be BIG and chunky like in the portrait - about as wide as his head, with a thin gold rim, sitting ON TOP of the fur collar. In the FRONT view it is on the right side of the image (his left shoulder); in the BACK view it is clearly visible on the left side of the image (his left shoulder); in the right-side view it is hidden behind his body. His right shoulder has NO pauldron, only the fur collar. The magenta sash stays exactly as it is, everything else stays exactly as it is.
