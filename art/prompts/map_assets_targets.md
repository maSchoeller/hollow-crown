## Gemeinsamer Rahmen der Prompts

> Create an image, portrait 9:16. The attached picture is a screenshot of my mobile tactics game: a square-grid board seen from an orthographic camera tilted 35 degrees from straight down. Repaint it as a target picture for the 3D level art. Keep the exact layout, grid scale, camera. Remove the small figures and all UI. Style: rounded, chunky low-poly toy diorama, hand-painted textures, professional studio quality. Keep small grid crosses at tile corners. No text, no people.

## ziel_paktwacht

> Bright warm late summer light, soft shadows. (1) The castle wall stops looking like flat floor slabs: clearly raised, warm sandstone, walkway and rounded battlements on top, darker shadowed front face, impassable at once. (2) One round chunky tower on each wall corner with a small pennant. (3) Where the wall has a gap of two tiles: one real two-tile gatehouse, two stout towers, wooden double gate open under a stone arch. (4) The rock area is one solid connected massif of grey-brown rock filling its tiles to the edge, stepped ledges, small tufts of grass, impassable. (5) Courtyard: packed earth and worn flagstones in calm beige instead of saturated orange. (6) Trees in groups of 2 to 3 at the tile edges, tile centre open.

## ziel_gewoelbe

> Dark but readable. (1) No grass, no sky light. Boulder areas become one solid connected mass of dark grey-violet cave rock to the tile edge, top rough and higher than the floor. (2) Corridors and chambers: worn flagstones in cool grey, lighter than the rock. (3) Warm torch light on the walls along the main corridor. (4) In the chambers, stone sarcophagi of young kings with small crown reliefs, cracked pillars, rubble. (5) A faint ember-coloured glow from the north end.

## ziel_moor

> Misty morning light. (1) The clear turquoise pool becomes a real moor with two water types: deep channels dark green-brown with lily pads, impassable; shallow swamp lighter muddy olive with reeds, mud banks and tussocks, wadable. (2) Plank blocks become slim wooden boardwalks on visible posts with a rope on one side. (3) The two stone towers become a poor refugee camp: patched canvas tents, drying racks, campfire, crates. (4) Soft shore edges with reeds and mud. (5) Islands: muted boggy green and dark earth.
