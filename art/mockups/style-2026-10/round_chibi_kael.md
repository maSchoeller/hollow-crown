## Prompt

> Create an image, 3:2 landscape format. Game art style sheet for a fantasy tactics RPG (Fire Emblem meets Battle Brothers), high fantasy, serious story with warm campfire humor. LEFT HALF: half-body dialogue portrait of KAEL, a 34-year-old mercenary captain: dark brown hair tied in a short low ponytail, light stubble, tired but kind amber eyes, a small scar through his left eyebrow, an old dented knight's breastplate with a scratched-off emblem, one big pauldron on his left shoulder only, ash-grey frayed cloak, ember-orange sash, longsword; warm, slightly wry smile, friendly and approachable. RIGHT HALF: a portrait-orientation phone screen mockup of the battle screen: a small 3D diorama battle map seen from about 35 degrees above, square grass tiles, a small river with a wooden bridge, a few trees and two cottages; five small chibi units about 2.5 heads tall: Kael, a young female archer and a big strong female axe fighter all wearing BLUE team cloth, and two bandits wearing RED cloth; a soft blue movement-range highlight on a few tiles. At the bottom of the phone screen a light bone-white (#F3EEE4) dialogue strip with a small round Kael face icon and grey placeholder bars instead of text, dark ink (#1F1E24) details and one ember-orange (#E8743B) button; a slim light HUD bar at the top. Clean, friendly, professional mobile game UI. No readable text, no labels, no logo, no watermark. Plain warm off-white background behind both halves. STYLE: 'Round Chibi Cartoon' - strongly simplified cartoon, big round heads even in the portrait, thick soft dark outlines, toy-like rounded forms, simple glossy shading, bright cheerful colors, like Kingdom Rush or Mario + Rabbids. Not photorealistic, not dark.

## Beobachtungen

- Ergebnis ist eher Anime-Chibi als Kingdom Rush: Outlines dünner als verlangt, Gesicht mit großen Augen.
- Porträt und Brett-Figuren teilen einen Stil → sehr gute Lesbarkeit auf dem Handy, passt zur hellen UI.
- Die Axtkämpferin ist nicht größer als die anderen; Silhouetten müssen die Identität tragen.
- Rüstung noch recht detailliert/verwittert → für die Serie vereinfachen.
