## Stil- und Formatvorgabe (erste Nachricht je Chat)

> Create an image. The attached images are the APPROVED STYLE REFERENCES for my original fantasy tactics RPG 'Hollow Crown' (characters are round chibi cartoon, the map is a bright toy-like 3D diorama). Now I need ENVIRONMENT / LOCATION concept art that fits this exact look: bright, warm, friendly stylised cartoon painting with soft rounded shapes, clean soft outlines, simple cel-like shading, saturated but warm colors, readable silhouettes, Ghibli-like warmth even in serious scenes; NOT photorealistic, NOT gritty, NOT dark painterly. Visual-novel dialogue background: widescreen landscape 16:9, no characters, no people, no text, no logos; important content in the central third; lower third calm. Location: …

Folgenachrichten: *Next location - same style and format (16:9, no people, no text). Location: …*
