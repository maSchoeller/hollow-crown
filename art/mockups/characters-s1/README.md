## Stil- und Formatvorgabe (erste Nachricht je Chat)

> Create an image. The two attached images (KAEL and RHEN) are the APPROVED STYLE REFERENCES for my original fantasy tactics RPG 'Hollow Crown'. I will ask for a series of character portraits, one image per message. For every portrait match exactly this STYLE: 'Round Chibi Cartoon' - big round head, large expressive eyes, soft dark-brown outlines, simple glossy cel shading, bright warm colors, rounded toy-like forms, friendly and readable; serious fantasy story with Ghibli-like warmth, never gritty or realistic. FORMAT for every portrait (like the RHEN reference): square 1:1, one single character, half-body dialogue portrait (head to hips), body turned slightly, centered, FULLY TRANSPARENT background (PNG with real alpha channel), no floor, no shadow, no text, no name, no logo, no frame. Members of the mercenary company 'Ashen Banner' wear a small ember-orange (#E8743B) cloth detail; enemies and nobles do not.

Folgenachrichten: *Next portrait - same style and format (square 1:1, half-body dialogue portrait, FULLY TRANSPARENT background with real alpha, no text). Character: …*
